◆ Key Discrimination Insight
What separates the cancer types spectroscopically

~990 cm⁻¹ (Phe): CRC highest, PRO/OVA lowest among cancers. Primary subtype separator.

~1350 cm⁻¹ (Trp/CH₂): CRC peaks here; LUN depleted. Separates the two largest cohorts.

~1547 cm⁻¹ (Amide II): Uniquely elevated in LUN — only subtype with positive shift.

~2085 cm⁻¹ (C≡N): OVA uniquely low, distinguishing it from PRO.

~1001 cm⁻¹ (shifted Phe): CPAN depleted slightly up-shifted from 990 peak.
